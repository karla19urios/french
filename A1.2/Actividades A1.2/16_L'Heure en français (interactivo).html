L'Heure en Français

Telling time in French — the two ways to count minutes, the 12-hour/24-hour conversion, and putting it all into your own daily routine.

🎯 Objectif

Be able to express the time in French — read a clock, ask and answer Quelle heure est-il ?, and describe your daily routine with times attached.

Note: this lesson uses the 24-hour clock throughout (like your slides — dix-neuf heures = 19:00), which is how time is usually told formally in French. One exception to watch for: 1:00 is une heure, not un heure — heure is a feminine word.

1

Compter les Minutes

Set an hour and a minute — watch the clock, and hear how the French changes. Up to the half hour, you count minutes forward; past it, French counts backward from the next hour.

Heure 19
Minutes 00
19:00

0–30 min: heure(s) + minutes (et quart at :15, et demie at :30) · 31–59 min: next hour moins the minutes left (moins le quart at :45) — both styles are correct, the "moins" style is just more common when speaking.
