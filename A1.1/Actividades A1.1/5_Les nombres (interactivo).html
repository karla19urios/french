Les Nombres

A lesson for learning to count in French — from zero to twenty, and a bit beyond.

⏱ 50 min 🎯 Level A1 🔢 0–20 (+ tens)
1

Learning objectives

By the end of class, students will be able to:

  • Recognize and correctly pronounce the numbers 0 to 20 in French.
  • Answer the question "Combien de...?" by counting objects out loud.
  • Identify the tens (30, 40, 50... 100) by sound and in writing.
2

Activity 1 — Flashcards and repetition (15 min)

The teacher says each number out loud and the class repeats in chorus. Then each student taps the cards to review pronunciation on their own.

3

Activity 2 — Matching game (20 min)

In pairs or individually: click a number, then click the French word that matches it. Classroom variant: split the group into two teams and turn it into a board competition.
