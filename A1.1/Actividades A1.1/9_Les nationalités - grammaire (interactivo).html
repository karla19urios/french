Les Nationalités

Say where you're from and what your nationality is in French — ten countries, the little word that changes before each one, and the adjective that agrees with you.

bonjour
du monde

The tricky part: "coming from" a country isn't always de — French picks de la, du, de l', or des depending on whether the country's name is feminine, masculine, starts with a vowel, or is plural.

de la→feminine country — la France → de la France
du→masculine country — le Mexique → du Mexique
de l'→starts with a vowel — l'Inde → de l'Inde
des→plural country — les États-Unis → des États-Unis

And nationality adjectives agree with you: most add an -e for a woman speaking (français → française), and words already ending in -e or -s can stay the same or double a consonant (chinois stays chinois).
