La Famille

Meet the family — learn the words for parents, siblings, grandparents and more, then put together a few sentences to introduce your own.

🎯 Objectif

Be able to present your family: say who each person is, and describe them with a couple of simple sentences.

🧩 The pattern

Your slides give you two sentence starters for every family member — this lesson fills them in with real words so you can practice out loud.

…est… (is)
gives their name or describes them — Mon père est Jean. / Mon père est gentil.
…a…ans (has…years)
gives their age — Mon père a 52 ans. (My father is 52.)

Note: mon (my, masculine) and ma (my, feminine) are used throughout — your next lesson covers the full set of possessive adjectives, so we're keeping to just these two for now.
