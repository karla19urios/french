How this lesson works

French has two ways to say "you" — it changes almost every question below. Use the switch that appears once you scroll down to flip the whole page between them.

Vous — formal Strangers, teachers, work, anyone older or in a position of respect. Also the "you-plural" for a group.
Tu — casual Friends, classmates, family, kids. Using tu with the wrong person can sound rude — when unsure, start with vous.
