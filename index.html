1 · Learning Rules

📋 Learning Rules

  • 1Practice a little every day — a few minutes daily beats one long session a week.
  • 2Listen before you read — let your ear catch the rhythm and sounds first.
  • 3Don't skip the exercises — actually producing French is what makes it stick.
  • 4Mistakes are part of learning — revisit any lesson as many times as you need.
2 · What to Expect

🔍 What to Expect

  • Levels go from A1 to B2, following the CEFR scale — start at your level and move up.
  • Each level is organized into Grammaire, Lectures, Audios (and Vidéo where available).
  • Lessons mix interactive activities with reference PDFs — click to open, right in your browser.
  • Nothing is locked — jump around, skip ahead, or go back whenever you like.
